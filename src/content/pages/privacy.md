## What this site collects

If you send the contact form, this site collects what you type into it: your name, email address, business name (if you give one), the offer you picked, and your message. It’s emailed to Ty so he can reply. It isn’t sold or shared for marketing.

This site doesn’t currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

## Services this site uses

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site and runs Cloudflare Turnstile on the contact form to block spam. To do that, Cloudflare processes basic request data such as your IP address.
- **Resend** delivers contact form messages by email.

## Questions

Email ty at paradoxcreative dot com to ask what information Paradox has about you, or to have it deleted.
