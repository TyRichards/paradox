_Last updated: November 2026._

If you use the contact form, this site collects your name, email, business name (if you give one), the offer you picked, and your message. It’s emailed to Ty so he can reply. It isn’t sold or shared for marketing.

This site doesn’t currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Cloudflare hosts the site and runs Turnstile on the form to block spam. Cloudflare may process basic request data, such as your IP address. Resend delivers the form messages by email.

Email ty at paradoxcreative dot com to ask what information Paradox has about you, or to have it deleted.
