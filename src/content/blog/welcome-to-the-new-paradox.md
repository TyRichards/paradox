## What a post can contain

Posts are plain Markdown files in `src/content/blog/`. Edit them in your editor or in Sveltia CMS at `/admin/`.

- Headings, lists, links, and images
- Code blocks and quotes
- Front matter for the title, description, date, tags, and draft status
